About Our Store

A Local Furniture Store That Actually Cares

When we started VV Gallery, we wanted to build something different: a real, down-to-earth furniture store where you're greeted with a smile, given straight answers, and charged fair prices.

We are everyday people who live in the area. We know how expensive furnishing a home can get, and we don't believe in high-pressure sales pitches or inflated price tags. Whether you need an affordable mattress starting at $198, a modular lovesac couch for family movie nights, or a lighted makeup vanity, we're here to help you find what fits your life and your budget.
