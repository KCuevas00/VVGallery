Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Straightforward answers about Ashley catalog orders, local freight handling, warranties, and financing.

Are these authentic Ashley Furniture pieces? ▾

Yes, 100%. As an authorized regional partner, VV Gallery orders directly through Ashley Furniture Industries’ authorized dealer freight network. Every piece comes with genuine manufacturer warranty coverage and original factory packaging.

How does pricing compare to Ashley HomeStores? ▾

Because we operate locally without expensive corporate mall overhead, we offer highly competitive catalog pricing. Plus, you get direct local delivery from Barber Greene Rd, saving both freight fees and the 40+ minute drive to Rockford or Elgin.

How long does shipping and delivery take? ▾

Most factory catalog orders reach our DeKalb location in 5 to 14 business days. When your shipment arrives, our team inspects every carton for defects and calls you immediately to schedule home delivery or free warehouse pickup.

Can I finance or lease-to-own an Ashley order? ▾

Yes! You can take advantage of our financing partners for any Ashley catalog order, including Synchrony Financial (0% APR promotional terms for qualifying buyers) and Acima (no credit needed lease-to-own with as little as $49 down). Explore financing options →

What is the return policy on catalog special orders? ▾

Pieces ordered specifically from manufacturer catalogs are final sale once processed and cannot be returned or exchanged. Our showroom team will help you verify measurements, fabrics, and left- or right-facing orientation before committing. See our refund policy.

Where do you deliver? ▾

We deliver across DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Rochelle, Genoa, and surrounding Kane/DeKalb County areas. Doorstep drop-off and room-of-choice setup are both available, or you can pick up for free at our showroom. See our delivery areas.
