Our Heritage & Purpose

Continuing the Legacy of Efraín Vázquez

We are a family-owned company with a story that began over 35 years ago, when the father of our family started his journey in the furniture industry with dedication, hard work, and passion.

The opening of our doors is a tribute to Efraín Vázquez, who devoted his life to the furniture industry and whose legacy is far from finished. His work, vision, and love for serving others planted a foundation that continues to grow.

Today, his daughter Vanessa Vázquez has chosen to step forward in faith and open our doors to continue that legacy, honoring the path Efraín built and carrying on the heritage he left behind.

With the wisdom, guidance, understanding, and prudence of God, we have decided to open our doors to the public with the same warmth, kindness, and grace that have always defined us, committed to providing excellent service and genuine care to every customer.

At Vane Vintage Gallery, we prepare each day to offer high-quality furniture, beautiful designs, and the best prices, with the purpose of helping you create spaces filled with comfort, style, and meaning.

✨ Thank you for being part of our story. Welcome to Vane Vintage Gallery.
