How ordering works

You pick the piece. We handle the ordering, the freight and the delivery, so you don’t have to drive to a big-box store.

Direct Factory Freight

Skip the 40-minute drive to Elgin or Rockford. We receive freight at our DeKalb warehouse, inspect every carton for damage, and coordinate local delivery directly to your home.

  1. Send us the item

    Browse Ashley online, then send us the item name, item number or a link. Or stop in and we’ll look it up together on our screens.

  2. We confirm stock and price

    We check availability and give you the price before you order. If you want to pay over time, we can go over financing too.

  3. We receive it and deliver it

    The order ships to our DeKalb location. We check it over when it arrives, call you, and set up a delivery day that works for you.

Before you order

100% authentic Ashley pieces
Every item is ordered directly through Ashley Furniture Industries’ authorized dealer network. Pieces arrive in genuine factory packaging with full manufacturer warranty protection.
How long it takes
Most catalog orders reach our DeKalb location in 5 to 14 business days. When yours arrives, we call you to schedule delivery. See our shipping policy.
Catalog orders are final sale
Pieces ordered from a manufacturer catalog can’t be returned or exchanged. Check the size, color and left or right facing with us before you commit. See our refund policy.
Paying over time
Acima lease-to-own and Synchrony financing are available. Acima needs no credit, just steady income and a checking account. See financing options.
Where we deliver
We deliver across DeKalb County and nearby towns, or you can pick up for free at our showroom. See delivery areas.
