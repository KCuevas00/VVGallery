Questions

How does ordering work?

Send us the item name, number or a link, or stop in and we’ll look it up together. We confirm stock and your price before you order, then the piece ships to our DeKalb location.

How long does it take?

Most catalog orders reach our DeKalb location in 5 to 14 business days. We check it over when it arrives and call you to schedule delivery. See our shipping policy.

Can I return a catalog order?

No. Pieces ordered from a manufacturer catalog are final sale, so check the size, color and left or right facing with us before you commit. See our refund policy.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. Acima lease-to-own and Synchrony financing are available. Acima needs no credit, just steady income and a checking account. See financing options.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across DeKalb County and nearby towns, or you can pick up for free at our showroom. See our delivery areas.
