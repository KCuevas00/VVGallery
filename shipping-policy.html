Shipping & Delivery Policy

Fast, professional, and reliable local delivery throughout DeKalb County and surrounding areas.

Fast Local Delivery from Our DeKalb Warehouse

Unlike big national chains that take 4 to 8 weeks to ship from distant distribution centers, VV Gallery stocks hundreds of pieces right here in DeKalb, IL for immediate scheduling and fast delivery.

1. Service Delivery Areas

We provide scheduled delivery throughout Northern Illinois, including:

  • Immediate Coverage: DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Malta, Genoa, Kingston.
  • Regional Coverage: Rochelle, Maple Park, Elburn, Kaneville, Hinckley, Waterman, Creston.
  • Extended Greater Chicagoland / Fox Valley: Available upon request with tailored delivery rates. Call us at (847) 306-0959 for an instant quote to your zip code.

2. Local Delivery & In-Store Pickup

We provide fast local delivery throughout DeKalb, Sycamore, and surrounding areas. For delivery rates, scheduling, or special setup inquiries, please speak with an associate in our showroom or give us a call.

  • Fast Local Delivery: Available across DeKalb County and nearby communities. Delivery dates and fees are confirmed directly at the time of purchase.
  • Free Customer Pickup: Free customer pickup is available at our showroom warehouse (1586 Barber Greene Rd, DeKalb, IL) during regular business hours (Mon–Fri 10:00 AM–6:30 PM, Sat 10:00 AM–5:00 PM).
  • For More Details: Talk to an associate in our showroom or call/text us anytime at (847) 306-0959.

3. Delivery Preparation Checklist

To ensure a smooth delivery day:

  • Please measure all doorways, hallways, stairwells, and turns to confirm your selected sofa or mattress set will fit.
  • Ensure walkways and driveways are clear of vehicles, snow, ice, or obstructions.
  • An adult 18 years or older must be present at the time of delivery to inspect and sign for the merchandise.

4. Ashley Furniture & Special Catalog Orders

For custom catalog items and partner orders (including Ashley Furniture pieces), items are shipped directly to our DeKalb hub and typically arrive within 5 to 14 business days. As soon as your order is received and quality-checked by our team, we will call you to arrange your preferred delivery date.
