Questions & Answers

Ashley Furniture Ordering FAQ

Are these authentic Ashley Furniture pieces?

Yes, 100%. As an authorized regional partner, VV Gallery orders directly through Ashley Furniture Industries' authorized dealer freight network. Every piece comes with its genuine manufacturer warranty and factory packaging.

How does pricing work compared to Ashley HomeStores?

Because we operate locally without giant corporate mall overhead, we offer very competitive pricing on catalog orders. You get the exact same piece with direct local delivery, saving you both delivery fees and the 40-minute drive to Rockford or Elgin.

How long does shipping take?

Factory in-stock items generally arrive at our DeKalb warehouse in 1 to 3 weeks. Once our team receives and inspects the freight, we call you immediately to schedule a delivery day and time that works with your schedule.

Can I finance an Ashley order?

Yes! You can take advantage of our financing partners for any Ashley special order, including Synchrony Financial (revolving credit with promotional terms) and Acima (no credit needed lease-to-own with as little as $1 to $49 down). Learn more about our financing options →

Prefer to Browse Together?

Stop by our DeKalb showroom on Barber Greene Rd. We can pull up the full Ashley catalog on our screens, compare cushion fabrics and finishes, and write up your quote in person.
